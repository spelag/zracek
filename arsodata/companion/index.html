Connect your ring

The ring only talks over Bluetooth while it is in setup mode.

  1. Hold the button while powering the ring on, or hold the button for about 2 seconds while a forecast is showing.
  2. Keep the ring close to your phone.
  3. Tap Connect and choose the ring in Chrome's device picker.
